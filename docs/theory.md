# Theoretical results used in the manuscript (with proofs)

Notation. n qubits; computational basis |b⟩, b ∈ {0,1}ⁿ; s_b ∈ {±1}ⁿ with (s_b)_j = 1 − 2b_j (the Z-eigenvalues).
Angle-encoding unitary D(x) = ⊗ⱼ RZ(xⱼ) = Σ_b e^{−i x·s_b/2} |b⟩⟨b|. Fidelity kernel K(x,x′) = |⟨φ(x)|φ(x′)⟩|².

## Proposition 1 (cancellation of post-encoding unitaries) — explains the failure of the previous design
Let |φ(x)⟩ = V E(x)|ψ₀⟩ where V is unitary and independent of x. Then K(x,x′) = |⟨ψ₀|E(x)†E(x′)|ψ₀⟩|² for all x, x′.
*Proof.* ⟨φ(x)|φ(x′)⟩ = ⟨ψ₀|E(x)†V†V E(x′)|ψ₀⟩ and V†V = I. ∎
**Corollary 1.1.** For E = D and |ψ₀⟩ = |+⟩^⊗n: K(x,x′) = ∏ⱼ cos²((xⱼ−x′ⱼ)/2). For |ψ₀⟩ = |0⟩^⊗n: K ≡ 1.
*Proof.* D(x)†D(x′) = D(x′−x) is a product of single-qubit diagonal unitaries; ⟨+|RZ(δ)|+⟩ = cos(δ/2); ⟨0|RZ(δ)|0⟩ = e^{−iδ/2}. ∎
Consequence: any "trainable" or "entangling" layer placed after the last data-dependent gate is invisible to the kernel; the previous manuscript's θ-grid and CNOT ring had exactly zero effect (verified numerically to 4×10⁻¹⁵).

## Proposition 2 (torus-covariant kernel with a fiducial state) — the primary construction
Let |ψ_θ⟩ = V(θ)|0⟩^⊗n be any fiducial state and |φ(x)⟩ = D(x)|ψ_θ⟩. Write p_b(θ) = |⟨b|ψ_θ⟩|² for its Z-basis distribution. Then

  K_θ(x,x′) = | Σ_b p_b(θ) e^{ i (x′−x)·s_b / 2 } |² = |χ_p((x′−x)/2)|²,

where χ_p(t) = E_{b∼p}[e^{i t·s_b}] is the characteristic function of p. In particular:
(a) **Covariance/invariance.** K_θ(x+h, x′+h) = K_θ(x,x′) for every h ∈ ℝⁿ: the kernel is invariant under the action of the torus group U(1)ⁿ (translations of all encoding angles), i.e. it is a covariant quantum kernel in the sense of Glick et al. for the group generated by {Z_j}.
(b) **Dependence on θ only through p.** Two fiducial states with the same Z-distribution give the same kernel; relative phases of |ψ_θ⟩ are irrelevant.
(c) **Product fiducial state ⇒ product kernel.** If p is a product distribution with marginals P(s_j=+1) = (1+m_j)/2, m_j = ⟨Z_j⟩, then K_θ(x,x′) = ∏ⱼ [cos²(δⱼ/2) + m_j² sin²(δⱼ/2)], δ = x′−x. Each factor is a per-feature bandwidth interpolation between cos² (m_j = 0) and the constant 1 (|m_j| = 1). With m ≡ 0 one recovers Corollary 1.1.
(d) **Entanglement.** K_θ is a non-product function of δ iff p is not a product distribution; since product states have product p, entanglement of the fiducial state is *necessary* (though not sufficient) for a non-product kernel.
(e) **Positive definiteness and Fourier structure.** K_θ(δ) = Σ_{b,b′} p_b p_{b′} cos((s_b − s_{b′})·δ/2) is a positive-definite shift-invariant kernel (Bochner) whose spectral measure is supported on the finite frequency set {(s_b − s_{b′})/2} ⊂ {−1,0,1}ⁿ, with non-negative weights p_b p_{b′}. Equivalently K_θ admits an explicit classical feature map of dimension ≤ 3ⁿ (cosines and sines of ω·x for ω ∈ {−1,0,1}ⁿ).

*Proof.* ⟨ψ_θ|D(x)†D(x′)|ψ_θ⟩ = Σ_b |⟨b|ψ_θ⟩|² e^{−i(x′−x)·s_b/2} (D is diagonal); take the modulus squared. (a) follows because only x′−x appears. (b) is immediate. (c): for product p the sum factorises over qubits; |P₊e^{iδ/2} + P₋e^{−iδ/2}|² = cos²(δ/2) + (P₊−P₋)² sin²(δ/2). (d): if p is a product distribution the kernel is a product; the contrapositive gives necessity; product states have product p, so an unentangled fiducial state cannot produce a non-product kernel. (e): expand |Σ_b p_b e^{iδ·s_b/2}|² = Σ_{b,b′} p_b p_{b′} e^{iδ·(s_b−s_{b′})/2}; the imaginary parts cancel pairwise. ∎

**Consequences for this study.** (i) The parameters θ and the entangling gates of V(θ) genuinely shape the kernel (they act *before* the data), curing the defect of Proposition 1. (ii) The kernel is "geometric" in a precise sense: it is the unique class of fidelity kernels covariant under the torus action of the RZ encoding, parameterised by the fiducial distribution p. (iii) For n = 8 the kernel is classically computable from the 2⁸ = 256 numbers p_b at cost O(2ⁿ) per entry, and admits a 3ⁿ = 6561-dimensional explicit feature map; the study therefore makes **no claim of quantum advantage** — it studies approximation methodology on a quantum-native kernel family that is exactly simulable at this width. For general n the cost O(2ⁿ) per kernel entry is exponential, but sampling p from the circuit and forming K̂ from samples is a classical estimator with the same shot statistics as the quantum inversion test, so this family is a poor candidate for advantage at any width; we state this openly.

## Proposition 3 (first-order propagation of estimation noise through the regularised Nyström inverse)
Setting. W ∈ ℝ^{m×m} symmetric PSD (exact landmark kernel), W_ε = W + εI with ε > 0, C ∈ ℝ^{N×m}, K̃ = C W_ε⁻¹ Cᵀ. Estimates Ĉ = C + ΔC, Ŵ = W + ΔW with ΔW symmetric. Assume ‖ΔW‖₂ < λ_min(W) + ε = 1/‖W_ε⁻¹‖₂ (so that Ŵ_ε = Ŵ + εI is invertible and the Neumann series converges).

(a) Expansion. K̃(Ĉ, Ŵ) − K̃(C, W) = ΔC W_ε⁻¹ Cᵀ + C W_ε⁻¹ ΔCᵀ − C W_ε⁻¹ ΔW W_ε⁻¹ Cᵀ + R, with ‖R‖_F = O(‖ΔC‖²) + O(‖ΔC‖‖ΔW‖) + O(‖ΔW‖²).

(b) Bound. ‖K̃(Ĉ,Ŵ) − K̃(C,W)‖_F ≤ 2‖C‖₂ ‖W_ε⁻¹‖₂ ‖ΔC‖_F + ‖C‖₂² ‖W_ε⁻¹‖₂² ‖ΔW‖_F + ‖R‖_F.

(c) Shot statistics (idealised inversion test, S shots per entry, independent entries). E‖ΔC‖_F² = Σ_{ik} C_ik(1−C_ik)/S ≤ Nm/(4S) and E‖ΔW‖_F² = Σ_{p≠q} W_pq(1−W_pq)/S ≤ m(m−1)/(4S) (the diagonal of W is known to be 1 and is not estimated).

*Proof.* (a) (W_ε + ΔW)⁻¹ = W_ε⁻¹ − W_ε⁻¹ ΔW W_ε⁻¹ + Σ_{k≥2} (−1)^k W_ε⁻¹(ΔW W_ε⁻¹)^k, convergent under the assumption; substitute Ĉ = C + ΔC and collect first-order terms. (b) Sub-multiplicativity ‖AB‖_F ≤ ‖A‖₂‖B‖_F and ‖W_ε⁻¹‖₂ = 1/(λ_min(W)+ε). (c) Var[Binomial(S,p)/S] = p(1−p)/S ≤ 1/(4S). ∎

**Reading, and what is and is not claimed.** (i) The bound is deterministic and first-order; the term in ΔW carries the *square* of ‖W_ε⁻¹‖₂, so estimation error in the landmark block is amplified by 1/(λ_min(W)+ε)². (ii) The bound is an *upper bound*; the empirical quantity reported in the experiments, A = ‖K̃(Ĉ,Ŵ) − K̃(C,W)‖_F / (‖ΔC‖_F + ‖ΔW‖_F), is a realised amplification ratio, not the bound, and the two should not be equated: A ≤ max(2‖C‖₂‖W_ε⁻¹‖₂, ‖C‖₂²‖W_ε⁻¹‖₂²) + o(1) is what (b) implies. (iii) The assumption ‖ΔW‖₂ < λ_min(W)+ε **fails** in the regime that matters most: for the low-rank kernels studied here λ_min(W) ≈ 0, so with ε = 10⁻⁶ the noisy Ŵ_ε is indefinite (its smallest eigenvalue is negative of order the shot noise, ≈ −0.1 to −1 at S = 100–1,000 in our data). Then the first-order expansion no longer applies, the Moore–Penrose pseudo-inverse inverts negative eigenvalues of magnitude ≪ 1 and the error is no longer small in any sense — this is the collapse observed in §5.6, and it is *outside* the regime of (b), which is why the empirical ratios (≈ 20–40) should be read as the signature of a breakdown, not as a check of the constant in (b). (iv) The remedies follow from (iii): restoring the assumption by making the applied inverse's smallest inverted eigenvalue positive and bounded away from zero — PSD projection (discard eigenvalues ≤ 0), relative truncation (discard eigenvalues ≤ τ λ_max), or a jitter ε that scales with the noise level σ_W√m (ε_adaptive = c·σ_W√m, since ‖ΔW‖₂ ≲ 2σ_W√m for an m×m symmetric matrix with i.i.d. entries of standard deviation σ_W) — bounds ‖Ŵ_ε⁺‖₂ and therefore the amplification. Normalising K̃ to unit diagonal rescales rows and columns but does not change the spectrum of the applied inverse, so it cannot repair the amplification; §5.6 confirms this.

## Proposition 4 (circuit-evaluation accounting)
For a symmetric fidelity kernel estimated by the inversion test, the exact Gram matrix on N training points requires N(N−1)/2 distinct circuit configurations (the diagonal is 1 by construction) and prediction on n_test points requires n_test·N more. Nyström with m landmarks requires m(m−1)/2 + N·m (training; N·m − m(m+1)/2 if the landmarks are training points) and n_test·m (test). Each configuration is repeated S times, so the shot-weighted workload is S times the count. For N = 600, m = 60, n_test = 2000 this gives 179,700 → 37,770 training evaluations (4.8×) and 1,200,000 → 120,000 test evaluations (10×). (The previous manuscript's N² = 360,000 double-counted symmetric pairs and ignored the test phase.)
