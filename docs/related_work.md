**Gap statement.** No prior work provides a controlled, statistically analysed comparison of Nyström-compressed quantum fidelity kernels against classical Nyström kernels at equal landmark budget, across landmark strategies, finite-shot noise, and multiple tasks — with a feature map whose parameters provably act on the kernel and with official test splits. That is what this study supplies, together with the theoretical characterisation (Props. 1–4) that explains what can and cannot be expected from such kernels at n = 8.
